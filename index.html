Plane Spotter

Point the top edge of your phone at the plane and hold still. It locks by itself.

Edit my view

  1. Drag a corner to move it. The chart is zoomed in on your outline, so small moves are precise.
  2. Or tap a corner and use the arrows: 0.5° per tap. On a computer, use the arrow keys (Shift for 2°).
  3. Add puts a new corner halfway along the next edge. Delete removes the selected corner.
  4. The amber dashes preview the cleanup. Press Done, clean up to save it.

Trace my view

  1. Stand where you usually watch, a step back from the metal frame.
  2. Hold the phone flat and sight along its top edge, from the middle of the bottom edge to the middle of the top edge.
  3. Aim at a corner where open sky meets a building or the window frame. Hold still and press Mark corner.
  4. Go around the edge of the open sky in order: down the building edge, along the rooftops, up the window frame, across the window top. Add a corner wherever the outline bends. 6–12 corners is plenty.
  5. Press Save view.

No compass? Tap the chart where each corner is.
